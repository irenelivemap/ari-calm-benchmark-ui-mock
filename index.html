How the route profiles are configured

Attribute Fast Calm Nature Calm Quiet

Calm Nature strongly prioritises trees, green space and water. Calm Quiet strongly avoids noise, main roads and areas with higher accident risk, while giving some additional value to nature.

The weights show how strongly each route attribute influences the profile. They range from 0 to 1: the higher the weight, the more influence that attribute has on which route is selected.
